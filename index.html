Getting there and home again

We fly in from London via Glasgow, and fly out from Islay Airport, each on our own way home.

The flight from Heathrow to Islay via Glasgow is budgeted at about R4,000 a person, one way. Exact times go in once we book. Getting to Heathrow, and the journey home from Islay Airport, are up to each of us.

An Taigh Our house

A crofthouse just outside Port Ellen

Six nights in a four-bedroom house on a working croft on the edge of Port Ellen, with a hot tub looking out across the island.

Check outFri 2 July

Inside and out

  • Open-plan kitchen, dining and living room with a wood-burning stove. Plenty of room for cooking together and long evenings.
  • Two bathrooms shared between the four bedrooms.
  • Six-person hot tub with views across the island. The favourite thing in almost every review.
  • Large garden for a barbecue, and parking at the back.
  • On a working croft, with a farm shop and a restaurant a few steps from the door. Guests mention deer grazing in the field behind.
  • Wi-fi and a TV, and free cancellation until 27 May 2027.

Getting into the village

The house sits between Port Ellen and the Machrie golf course. Port Ellen is about a 20-minute walk along the main road, which has no pavement, or under 5 minutes by taxi. In late June it stays light until after 10pm, but wear something bright on the road.

Mus fhalbh sinn Before we go

Good to know

Pack

  • A proper waterproof and warm layers. Summer here is changeable.
  • Walking shoes for the Three Distilleries Pathway.
  • Midge repellent for still evenings.
  • Some cash for buses and small cafés. The Co-op in Port Ellen covers the grocery shop.
  • Room in your bag for bottles.

Drams

  • Ask for driver's drams to take away if you'd rather pace yourself.
  • Two or three distilleries a day is plenty.
  • Eat lunch. Ardbeg's courtyard food and the Ardnahoe café are the best stops.
  • Distillery-only bottlings make the best souvenirs.

Travel

  • Loganair allows 15 kg in the hold plus 6 kg in the cabin. Leave room for bottles, or post them home.
  • Weather can delay island flights. The ferry is the backup, and Thursday stays flexible.
  • Opening days change, so recheck before booking tours.
  • Phone signal is patchy in the north.
