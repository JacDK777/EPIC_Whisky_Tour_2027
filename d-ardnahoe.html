Seanchas · The story

The new kid on the Sound

Ardnahoe was built by the Laing family of the independent bottler Hunter Laing, who found the four-acre site above Ardnahoe Loch in 2015. The first spirit ran in 2018.

Legendary Islay distiller Jim McEwan, who had run Bowmore and brought Bruichladdich back to life, came out of retirement to set up production.

It's the only distillery on Islay to cool its spirit in worm tubs: long copper coils sitting in tanks of cold water. It's slower and less efficient, but it gives a heavier, more textured spirit. The two copper stills have unusually long lyne arms, and the barley is milled on a 100-year-old Boby mill, the only second-hand kit in the place.

Look out for: Ardnahoe Loch just behind the distillery, where the little rowing boats sit on the water, and the CalMac ferry crossing the Sound to Port Askaig below.

The distillery

Islay's youngest working distillery, opened in 2018 above the Sound of Islay. Its long worm tubs cool the spirit slowly, and the visitor centre has one of the best views on the island.

House style. Young and still finding its voice: peated, fruity and fuller than you might expect.

Ardnahoe is new, so its range is still growing. Expect changes by 2027.

Ar turas · Our tour

Tasting Flight

WhenWed 30 June, 15:00
Length1½ hours
Priceabout £30 each
In randabout R700

The last stop of the north-coast day. Ardnahoe only runs its distillery tours on Tuesdays and Thursdays, so on Wednesday we sit down to a tasting flight instead, with the whole Sound of Islay and the Paps of Jura outside the window.

  • A flight of Ardnahoe, Islay's newest-built distillery, with its classically peated spirit matured in bourbon and sherry casks.
  • The view. The visitor centre looks straight across the Sound to Jura. Reviewers call the setting beautiful.
  • Café first. The café and bar serve coffee, cakes, scones and savoury snacks until 16:00. Arrive by 14:30 for a late lunch before the tasting.
  • Drivers can take their drams away.

18 and over only. The price isn't published yet, so we've estimated £30 each. This is Ardnahoe's 2026 timetable, which they say may change from July, so check when the 2027 dates come out. Open Tuesday to Saturday, 09:30 to 17:00. Book online or call 01496 840777.
