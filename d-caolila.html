Seanchas · The story

The quiet giant of Islay

Caol Ila was founded in 1846 by Hector Henderson, in a cove just north of Port Askaig where the Sound of Islay runs fast between Islay and Jura.

In the early 1970s the whole distillery except the warehouses was knocked down and rebuilt, which is why the still house is a modern block with huge windows. Today it's the biggest producer on Islay, and most of its spirit goes into blends, above all Johnnie Walker.

In August 2022 Diageo opened its new visitor centre as part of a £185 million investment in Scotch whisky tourism. It added the story rooms, a new shop and a bar with floor-to-ceiling windows over the Sound.

Look across the water: the three rounded peaks on Jura are the Paps of Jura. You'll see them all day as we drive up the Sound to Bunnahabhain and Ardnahoe.

The distillery

The largest distillery on Islay, tucked into a cove beside Port Askaig. Much of its spirit goes into blends, but the single malt is a smoky favourite, and the stillhouse has the best view on the island.

House style. Clean, oily smoke with lemon, pepper and a briny edge. Smoky but elegant.

Ar turas · Our tour

Flavour Journey Tour

✓ Included in our R29,000 trip budget
WhenWed 30 June, 10:00
Length1½ hours
Price£21 each
In randabout R500

A great-value start to a three-distillery day. You go from a story room full of island smells to the still house, which has the best view on Islay, and finish with a tasting looking straight across the Sound to the Paps of Jura.

  • The story room. A sound-and-light show about Caol Ila and Johnnie Walker, where you smell the peat, sea air and sweetness that shape the whisky.
  • The mash room and still house. Six stills behind floor-to-ceiling windows that frame Jura across the water.
  • The tasting: three tutored drams, then a small cocktail in the bar. One reviewer called his last dram "maybe the best I will ever have".
  • The shop has a fill-your-own section with a distillery-only cask.

Up to 14 people per booking. You can cancel or rebook up to 48 hours before. Caol Ila is closed on Fridays and Saturdays, so Wednesday works well. The production area has steep stairs. Opening hours March to October are Sunday to Thursday, 10:00 to 17:00.

What visitors say

  • The new visitor centre, which opened in 2022, gets called "fantastic".
  • The still house windows and the view of Jura from the tasting table.

Worth knowing

  • The story room uses lights, music and effects, which may not suit everyone.
  • Steep stairs in the production area. There's a video of that part in the bar for anyone who can't manage them.
  • The shop is tempting, so budget for a bottle.
