Seanchas · The story

The Progressive Hebridean Distillers

Bruichladdich was built in 1881 by the Harvey brothers, on the shore of Loch Indaal. Much of its Victorian kit is still working, including the open-topped cast-iron mash tun from 1881.

It was mothballed in 1994. In 2000 a group of independent investors led by wine merchant Mark Reynier bought it, legendary Islay distiller Jim McEwan came over from Bowmore to run production, and the stills came back to life in 2001. Rémy Cointreau bought it in 2012.

Bruichladdich makes three whiskies. The Bruichladdich range is unpeated and built on Scottish barley, some of it grown on Islay farms. Port Charlotte is heavily peated, at around 40 ppm, and is named after the village down the road. Octomore is the extreme one, with some releases peated past 300 ppm.

It also makes The Botanist gin, flavoured with 22 botanicals hand-foraged on Islay and distilled in a squat old Lomond still nicknamed Ugly Betty.

While you're there: on a fine day, take a dram from the shop to the shore across the road. The view back over Loch Indaal to Bowmore is one of the best on the island.

The distillery

The progressive Hebridean distillery. It works with Scottish barley farms, uses Victorian equipment, and makes three very different whiskies under one roof, plus The Botanist gin.

House style. The house style is unpeated: floral, barley-sweet and fruity. Port Charlotte and Octomore are the smoky side.

Ar turas · Our tour

Warehouse Tasting

✓ Included in our R29,000 trip budget
WhenTue 29 June, 14:00
Length1½ hours
Price£40 each
In randabout R900

The tasting reviewers keep recommending. You sit among the casks in the warehouse and taste whisky drawn straight from the barrel, with a host who knows every one of them.

  • Straight from the cask. The drams are drawn with a valinch, the long copper pipe used to sample a cask. One visitor's line-up was a 26-year-old Bruichladdich, a Port Charlotte from a Bordeaux wine cask and a Sauternes-finished Octomore.
  • All three styles under one roof: unpeated Bruichladdich, heavily peated Port Charlotte, and Octomore, the most heavily peated whisky in the world.
  • The shop afterwards sells Octomore and Port Charlotte releases you can't buy online. It has often had a distillery-only bottle you fill yourself from a cask, so ask.
  • Drivers get samples to take away.

18 and over only. Book two or three weeks ahead for summer, and you can cancel for a full refund up to 24 hours before. There's no café or restaurant, so eat in Bowmore before the bus. Open every day from March to October, 09:45 to 17:30. The bus home leaves at about 16:40, so we finish with time for the shop.

What visitors say

  • Knowledgeable, friendly guides with good stories. Katrina and Eva get named.
  • Warehouse tastings straight from the cask, with hosts who love what they do.
  • A homely, down-to-earth feel compared with the bigger names.
  • A good spread, from unpeated whisky to The Botanist gin.

Worth knowing

  • No café or restaurant on site. Picnic spots along Loch Indaal are the alternative.
  • No flash photography in the distillery.
  • The warehouses are cool, so bring a layer.
